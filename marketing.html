Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from restaurant owners about direct online ordering and BhojanOS.

What is BhojanOS?

BhojanOS is an all-in-one direct online ordering platform and restaurant management software for food businesses. It gives you a branded ordering website, digital QR menus, kitchen display system (KDS), inventory management, delivery dispatch, and customer CRM — with 0% marketplace commission.

How does direct ordering differ from food marketplaces like Zomato and Swiggy?

Third-party aggregators charge 18% to 28% commission per order and do not share customer phone numbers or names with you. With BhojanOS, you own the customer relationship, keep 100% of order revenue, and have direct access to your customer database for WhatsApp and SMS marketing.

Can restaurants have their own online ordering system and website?

Yes. BhojanOS creates a dedicated, mobile-friendly online ordering website under your restaurant brand. You can share this link on your Google Business Profile, Instagram bio, WhatsApp, and printed flyers.

How can restaurants get more direct orders?

Restaurants shift customers to direct ordering by placing QR codes on dine-in tables, including direct ordering flyers in delivery bags with a first-order discount, adding their direct link to Google Maps, and using WhatsApp marketing to re-engage repeat customers.

Can small restaurants and cloud kitchens in India use BhojanOS?

Absolutely. BhojanOS is built for Indian food businesses of all sizes — from single-outlet cloud kitchens and cafes to multi-branch restaurants. It supports UPI payments, FSSAI licensing display, and flexible local delivery options.

How are online payments processed?

BhojanOS integrates with Razorpay, allowing your customers to pay using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI, credit/debit cards, and Net Banking. Payments settle directly into your bank account with zero platform commission deductions.

How does delivery management work?

You have full control over delivery. You can use your own delivery riders, assign third-party delivery partners (like Dunzo, Shadowfax, or Porter), or offer customer takeaway/pickup. You can also configure delivery radius and distance-based fees.

Can I run BhojanOS alongside Swiggy and Zomato?

Yes! Most restaurants start by running BhojanOS alongside aggregators. Aggregators can provide initial discovery, while BhojanOS converts those diners into high-margin direct repeat customers over time.
